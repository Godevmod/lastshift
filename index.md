# Privacy Policy — Sys Admin: Last Shift

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026  
**Developer:** Godevmode  
**Privacy contact:** [ilya@godevmod.com](mailto:ilya@godevmod.com)

Godevmode operates **Sys Admin: Last Shift**, an Android game intended for players aged **16 and older**. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

## Information stored by the game

The game stores your best score locally on your device so it can remain available between sessions. The current game does not require a player account and does not upload your scores to a server operated by Godevmode.

The game also displays local technical information about advertising errors to help troubleshoot problems. This diagnostic screen does not automatically send its contents to us. If you choose to send us a screenshot or support message, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, message, app version, and error details.

## Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including full-screen interstitial advertisements after a lost shift.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares information such as IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The processing of information may depend on your location, consent choices, device settings, and advertising configuration.

For more information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Preserve your best score on your device.
- Provide and measure advertising.
- Detect fraud and maintain advertising service security.
- Diagnose technical problems.
- Respond to support requests you choose to send.

## Consent and privacy choices

Where applicable, the game presents a privacy message before requesting advertisements.

When available, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices through **AD PRIVACY OPTIONS** in the main menu. You may also manage your advertising identifier through your Android device's privacy settings.

Choosing not to allow personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements or prevent all advertising-related data processing.

## Sharing of information

The game's advertising integration sends information directly to Google and advertising partners involved in delivering advertisements, as described above.

If you contact us by email, the email services used to send, deliver, and store that correspondence also process its contents. We use the information you provide to address your request. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect legal rights and prevent abuse.

## Data retention and deletion

Your best score remains in local app storage until that storage is cleared. You can remove it through your Android device's app settings by clearing the game's storage. Device backups and restoration may also affect whether local data is restored after reinstallation, depending on your device settings.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to address your request, handle related follow-up issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed for those purposes. You can contact [ilya@godevmod.com](mailto:ilya@godevmod.com) to request deletion of information you have provided directly to us, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Google controls retention and deletion of information processed through its services under its own policies. Clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it does not itself delete information already held by Google.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. The game uses Android's app storage mechanisms for locally saved scores and does not send those scores to a Godevmode server. Access to support information is limited to what is needed to respond to requests, maintain the service, or meet legal obligations.

Third-party services apply their own security measures, as described in their policies. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Children and intended audience

Sys Admin: Last Shift is intended for players aged **16 and older** and is not directed to children under 16.

The game does not request a date of birth or require a player account. The stated audience does not mean that the game verifies each player's age. Advertising services may process device-related information as described in this policy.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children under 16. If a parent or guardian believes that a child under 16 has provided personal information directly to Godevmode, please contact [ilya@godevmod.com](mailto:ilya@godevmod.com). We will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete information under our control, unless retention is required by law. Information held by Google is subject to Google's privacy policies and request procedures.

Players aged 16 or 17 may still be minors under local law. Where parental or guardian authorization is required by applicable law, it must be obtained before providing personal information to us.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your personal information, object to processing, request a copy of your information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

To make a request concerning information held by Godevmode, contact [ilya@godevmod.com](mailto:ilya@godevmod.com). We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify and process your request. For information controlled by Google, consult the privacy resources linked above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
